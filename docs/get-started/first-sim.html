Connectivity Tutorial

First SIM

Order, activate, set the APN, then monitor. Registering a router is a different job.

Lexicon

Activate is a Service plan / subscription decision. Enable is a PIN, STK, or module feature switch. Attach is radio / packet attach on the module. A System in the API is not the same object as a SIM. Operator prose uses Service plan; the API keeps offer / offerId — map that once, do not rename the contract.

Before you start (SW Advanced SIM)​

SW Advanced and SW Essentials are Smart Connectivity.

SW Advanced needs STK active, automatic network selection, and packet-data auto-attach. First start-up scan is about 15 minutes with no data, SMS, Voice, or reboot. PIN is disabled by default.

SW Essentials and Enhanced carrier connectivity SIMs have no extra module requirements.

Details: First attach.

Order, activate, APN, monitor​

1

Order SIMs

You can order SIM cards directly from your AirVantage account, once your account is commercial. During connectivity trial phase, test SIMs are automatically sent to you upon sales request.

  1. Sign in at the host for your region: https://na.airvantage.net/accounts or https://eu.airvantage.net/accounts
  2. Follow the Order link
  3. Select the product you want to order
  4. Confirm delivery address
  5. Track the progress of the shipment from the order details screen

You will receive an email notification when SIMs are shipped.

Full job: Order SIMs.

2

Activate the service on your SIMs

Activate the service on the SIMs by selecting a Service plan. Radio attach happens after the APN is configured on the module.

  1. Sign in at the host for your region: https://na.airvantage.net/accounts or https://eu.airvantage.net/accounts
  2. Follow the Manage my connectivity link
  3. Select the SIM cards you want to use
  4. Click Activate, pick a Service plan, then click Activate

Enhanced carrier connectivity (Verizon) SIMs need an IMEI associated before activation. See How to activate an Enhanced Carrier Connectivity Verizon SIM.

Full job: Activate SIMs.

3

Configure the APN

SW Advanced and SW Essentials SIM​

To know which APN to configure, once the SIM is activated, go to Systems > Details and check the authorized APN in the System Info widget. In the documented example, only internet.swir is authorized on the system and shall be configured on the device or module.

If your Service plan specifies a different APN, it will appear there and will have to be configured in your module with the same value.

Operator prose uses Service plan. The API field remains offer / offerId.

Enhanced carrier connectivity SIM​

APNs for Enhanced carrier connectivity SIMs are pre-defined and do not depend on the Service plan picked at activation:

SW Advanced and SW Essentials alias Smart Connectivity. Enhanced carrier connectivity aliases Carrier+.

SMS short codes (Enhanced carrier connectivity)​

Enhanced carrier connectivity SIM only allows SMS from and to your application. For Mobile Originating SMS, program the device to send SMS to the carrier short code:

ProductShort code
AT&T-CC53340005
Verizon2183500292
T-Mobile3339
Rogers600000012

Messages sent to those short codes are transmitted to your application through AirVantage Cloud Connectors. For Mobile Terminated SMS, use the Send SMS action from the user interface or from the API.

Reference page: APN.

4

Monitor your connectivity

Track airtime usage of your SIMs and configure alerts.

From your AirVantage account, follow Manage my connectivity. The Dashboard shows how many SIMs are activated versus inventory or retired, how many are within the plan or exceeding/nearing plan, and month-to-date cumulated data usage.

Create your own rule from Configure > Alert rules.
