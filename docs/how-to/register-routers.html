ALMS How-to

Register routers

A separate device-management job. Registering a router does not activate its SIM subscription.

Before you begin​

AirLink Management Service (ALMS) supports current generation and legacy AirLink gateways and routers.

Current generation (ES, GX, LS): serial number
ES450, GX450, MP70, RV50/RV50X, RV55, LX60, LX40: serial number and IMEI
XR Series and AirLink OS: serial number and registration code
Legacy (Raven, PinPoint and MP with ALEOS 4.0.8 or newer): ESN or IMEI

This information is printed and bar coded on the device label. ALEOS devices also display it in ACEmanager.

XR Series routers (AirLink XR80, AirLink XR90) need an ALMS account that supports them. An older ALMS account does not register those devices; create a new ALMS account or ask Sierra Wireless to convert the existing one. Use a different email than the current ALMS login if you create a new account.

The AirLink device initiates communications and the platform responds with queued operations (heartbeat). Default heartbeat is once a day (1440 minutes). ALMS is a cloud-hosted service and requires Internet access from the device.

Register​

1

Sign in

Log into your ALMS account using the credentials sent during account creation.

2

Open Register

From the Start page dashboard, click the Register widget.

3

Identify the router

Enter the device serial number — the page detects the device type and adds the appropriate fields.

4

Complete the extra fields

For LWM2M ALEOS devices, complete IMEI/ESN. For AirLink OS, complete Registration Code. For MSCI, complete Password and Static IP/Host. Optionally add a name.

Registration of a new AirLink gateway or router (AirLink LX, ES, GX, RV and MP) includes one year of AirLink Complete. Register within 60 days of purchase.

warning

If your device communicates with ALMS using MSCI, enter the ACEmanager password from the device. Sierra Wireless does not recommend using MSCI; new devices use LWM2M by default.

Pre-configure​

You can pre-designate firmware version, configuration template, communication intervals and scheduled reports so ALMS applies them on first connect.

Check connectivity separately​

Different jobs, different evidence

Use the SIM activation flow for connectivity. Use device-management evidence to confirm router registration.

Registration creates the system record. The router still has to start contact.

After register, the device must ping and DNS-resolve na.airvantage.net, bs.airvantage.net, and lw.na.airvantage.net (Community articles, 2025-06-09 and 2025-09-17). If the IP answers and the name does not, that is DNS. On a private APN, open the firewall as in Configure infrastructure for a private APN.

Until first contact, ALMS can show System Type as the hardware name (example: AirLink XR90). After the router reports, that label becomes the OS variant (example: AirLink OS XR90). Community article (2026-02-12).

Firmware, AirLink OS release notes, and router bulletins stay on The Source.

Register promptly

Unregistered devices that still have AirLink Management Service enabled can be claimed by another AirVantage account. Register the router, or disable ALMS on devices you are not bringing to ALMS. Source: SWI-PSA-2023-002 (2023-05-12, CVE-2023-31279).

If the router still does not appear after DNS and WAN work, the Community steps walk ACEmanager Services > ALMS protocol reset: Troubleshooting AirLink devices not connecting to ALMS and Router not communicating to AirVantage.
